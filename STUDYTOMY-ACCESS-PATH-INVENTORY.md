## Boundary as inspected 2026-11-16

The Studytomy model/service tree inspected on 2026-11-16 had no managed-course marker and no purchase-backed course-grant resolver. Existing LearnHouse resource access still controlled public/published courses, authors, organization roles, user groups, and the upstream paid-access hook. A future managed course therefore had to be protected across every path below before it could be advertised. Do not add a blanket restriction to ordinary Community courses.

TASK-024 also depends on confirmed-collection work (TASK-021), learner eligibility (TASK-009), and business events (TASK-004). The order/collection and identity-policy decisions remain unresolved. This inventory does not authorize a grant, sale, learner access, or production migration.

## Completion criteria for TASK-025/TASK-026

1. Add the managed-resource marker, grant resolver, and shared reader policy only after their dependent collection, eligibility, and policy contracts exist.
2. Route every learner-facing course tree, direct activity, file, media, assignment, AI/RAG, token, and collaboration access through that policy while preserving upstream checks.
3. Retain the authenticated actor separately from the actual learner for delegated integrations; deny managed writes unless the target learner independently satisfies access, assessment, and deadline rules.
4. Add route-level regression cases for mismatched parent/course/activity IDs, direct file URLs and Range/HEAD, grant revocation during cache/stream use, token submit-on-behalf, and alternate exports/tools.
5. Verify ordinary unmanaged public/published Community courses retain their current behavior. No production activation while the applicable D-01/D-03/D-05/D-07/D-09/D-10/D-11/D-12 gates remain open.

This is a static inventory, not a complete access review or runtime acceptance result. Unreviewed surfaces above remain open work.
