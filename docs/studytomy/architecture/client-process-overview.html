Chapter 1 · System Boundary & Educational Scope

Two Separate Doors, One Dedicated Learning Workspace

To keep student records private and leave existing public marketing pages untouched, the public website and the learning portal operate as completely separate spaces.

Unchanged Public Website

studytomy.com / www.studytomy.com

Remains a standalone public brochure and general inquiry website with its own separate sign-ins and records.

  • Introduces Studytomy programmes to prospective students and parents
  • Handles general public inquiries and informational pages
  • Has zero automatic connection to private lessons, grades, or family records inside the learning portal
Kept Strictly Separate
Dedicated Learning Portal

lms.studytomy.com

The private, sign-in school workspace built for structured teaching, guided practice, and verified family progress reporting.

  • Houses approved syllabus PDFs, structured courses, videos, quizzes, science labs, and assignments
  • Provides source-cited AI study help and topic checks strictly within each learner's permissions
  • Protects student identity, consent choices, teacher reviews, certificates, and guardian reports in one controlled workspace

Tailored to the Sri Lankan National School Syllabus (English Medium)

Academic structure aligns with the National Institute of Education (NIE) and Ministry of Education stages in English instruction, locking each course to a specific syllabus edition and student cohort (for example, keeping 2015/2017 cohorts cleanly separated from an upcoming Grade 6 curriculum reform cohort).

Junior Secondary · Grades 6–9 Foundational Mathematics, Science, English, and ICT concepts with guided topic checks, visual explanations, and protected dependent-student accounts.
G.C.E. O/L Stage · Grades 10–11 Structured syllabus coverage, prerequisite diagnostic checks, cited topic exploration, objective quizzes, and teacher-marked written assignments.
G.C.E. A/L Stage · Grades 12–13 Advanced subject streams, interactive laboratory simulations (starting with the Momentum pilot), concept-to-page syllabus comparison, and student-controlled report sharing.

How Students Know Whether an Answer Is Officially in Their Syllabus

When students search Explore Topics across approved reference PDFs, every cited page carries an honest syllabus-coverage badge based strictly on educator-reviewed page mappings—never guesswork:

Confirmed in Selected Syllabus

An independent educator verified that this exact PDF page maps directly to a concept in the student's selected official syllabus edition.

Mapped to Another Approved Syllabus

The page belongs to an approved official syllabus in the library, but for a different grade or edition than the one currently selected.

Supplementary Reference

Approved by educators as helpful supporting study material, without claiming it is an official NIE syllabus requirement.

Unverified Coverage

The document is approved for reading, but no page-specific curriculum mapping has been signed off for this exact page yet.

Chapter 4 · Classroom Structure & Student Services

How Courses, Science Labs, Enrolments & Certificates Work

Beyond individual lessons, the portal organizes complete subjects, hands-on scientific simulations, seat allocations, and verifiable completion awards.

How Learning Material Is Organized

Level 1 · School Workspace Organization & Cohort

Separates each school or programme workspace, its staff permissions, approved policies, and syllabus editions.

Level 2 · Subject Container Course & Frozen Release

Groups a full subject (such as Grade 10 Science) with controlled drafts for teachers and frozen approved releases for students.

Level 3 · Study Sequence Ordered Chapters

Arranges topics in teaching order, supporting scheduled release dates (drip) and prerequisite or group locks.

Level 4 · Daily Tasks Lesson & Assessment Activities

Reading pages, searchable PDFs, captioned videos, collaborative boards, topic checks, science labs, quizzes, and assignments.

Hands-On Science

Virtual Science Laboratories (Momentum Pilot)

Students don't just read formulas—they run structured experiments where real physics laws drive every measurement:

  • Students adjust trolley masses and starting velocities on an interactive collision track
  • Verified physics equations—never AI text guesses—compute pre- and post-collision motion
  • Students record structured observations and submit their lab trial for scoring and educator review
Enrolment Integrity

Course Readiness, Seat Allocation & Re-Entry

Commercial and enrolment steps protect families from incomplete courses or lost academic history:

  • Readiness Checklist: A course cannot be offered until it is published, staff owners are named, and required policies are approved
  • Named Learner Seats: Orders bind each seat to a specific student and exact offer terms; unused seats can be corrected cleanly with an audit trail
  • Safe Re-Entry: If a student re-enrols in a later term, a fresh access period opens without erasing their earlier grades or completion history
Verifiable Recognition

Certificates With Correction & Withdrawal History

Awards carry real weight because they are tied directly to verified student work:

  • Authorized staff issue certificates only after confirming the learner's verified completion record for that course
  • Only the award holder (and authorized staff) sees private stored certificates—other students cannot browse them
  • If a grade appeal or marking review later amends a result, staff can issue a tracked correction or withdrawal while keeping the full lineage intact
Accountable Care

Confidential Support, Privacy & Safeguarding Cases

Sensitive family, privacy, or safeguarding matters are handled outside ordinary class chats:

  • Each case is claimed by a single accountable staff owner within that exact scope—preventing bystander snooping or duplicate handling
  • Independent students can request a bounded export of their own completion records through a verified workflow
  • Active case holds automatically block routine cleanup from deleting linked files until reviewed

Chapter 7 · Academic Integrity & Child Protection

Eight Non-Negotiable Quality & Safety Commitments

Studytomy enforces clear boundaries between helpful digital tools and genuine educator or family authority.

Principle How Studytomy Operates What Studytomy Never Allows
1. Lesson Completion vs. Mastery Tracks reading and viewing progress separately from verified diagnostics, quizzes, and educator-reviewed work. Never claims a student has mastered a syllabus topic just because they clicked through all pages of a chapter.
2. AI Question Drafts vs. Official Exams Lets teachers generate editable draft quiz questions tied to exact PDF quotes for faster lesson preparation. Never represents AI-drafted quizzes as official NIE / Department of Examinations G.C.E. O/L or A/L papers.
3. Objective Marking vs. Written Work Automatically scores clear multiple-choice and exact numeric answers using educator-approved rules. Never lets AI issue final formal grades on essays, worked proofs, speaking tasks, or practical observations.
4. Science Simulations vs. AI Guessing Calculates laboratory motion and measurements (such as the Momentum collision lab) using tested physics formulas. Never uses an AI text model to invent or estimate scientific experiment numbers.
5. Two-Person Content Sign-Off Requires a second, independent authorized educator to verify source permissions and approve syllabus mappings or managed releases. Never lets an uploader self-approve copyright permissions or publish unreviewed AI drafts directly to protected courses.
6. Payment vs. Family Authority Verifies guardian authority or older-student sharing consent independently before unlocking academic progress reports. Never grants an adult access to a child's academic reports or privacy choices merely because that adult paid an invoice.
7. Protected Minor Privacy Keeps younger students' profiles and class discussion posts private by default, and checks AI consent per purpose. Never exposes protected learners in public member directories or sends their work to external AI services without approved policy and consent.
8. Permanent Audit & Retention Safety Preserves an uneditable record of approvals, grade revisions, consent choices, and certificate versions, and blocks accidental deletion of held records. Never overwrites past course releases, erases historical learning evidence during course re-entry, or deletes safeguarded case files without review.

What Is Ready Today vs. What Happens Before Launch

The portal is currently verified in a controlled development workspace—not yet opened to live student traffic—so curriculum rights, educator reviews, and child-privacy policies are signed off in the right order before launching lms.studytomy.com.

  • Reviewed fractions Topic Check loop (check → cited reteach → new recheck) plus 30-minute local rough-work recovery
  • Adaptive curriculum/diagnostic foundation, pilot Momentum virtual science lab, family-link & consent records, and automated quality/security checks on every update
Human Sign-Offs Before Live Use

Pre-Launch Governance Checklist

  • Pilot Syllabus Selection: Confirm the exact initial grade(s), subject(s), NIE syllabus edition, and student cohort
  • Source Rights Clearance: Record publisher / NIE permission references for each official PDF uploaded
  • Educator Content Acceptance: Subject-specialist sign-off on curriculum mappings, lesson releases, and marking rubrics
  • Child & Family Policies: Owner approval of privacy notices, guardian identity verification steps, data retention periods, and AI consent terms
  • Commercial Terms: Confirm pricing currency (such as LKR), refund rules, and end-to-end pilot walkthrough sign-off with representative accounts
Planned Future Enhancements

Post-Pilot Expansion

  • Scanned Document Reading: Optical text recognition so older image-only scanned PDFs can be prepared before upload
  • Broader Topic Checks & Labs: Expanding interactive topic checks across more subjects and adding new validated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs
  • Managed Young-Learner Logins: Simplified login and recovery options for younger students without personal email addresses once child-account policies are approved
  • Live Classes & Exam Marking: Tutor class scheduling, attendance tracking, and structured step-by-step / handwritten answer evaluation
  • Hosted Production Launch: Controlled cloud hosting and backup operations on lms.studytomy.com
