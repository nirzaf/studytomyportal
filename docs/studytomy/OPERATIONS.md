# Studytomy report delivery recovery

The report scheduler is off by default. Enable `LEARNHOUSE_STUDYTOMY_REPORT_DELIVERY_ENABLED=true` only in the isolated test API for a synthetic rehearsal. Live operation requires named owners, approved disclosure policies, and service targets under TASK-069. No availability, response, or recovery target has been approved in this record.

## When an outbox lease expires

The next poll moves the expired lease to `reconciliation_required` with `LEASE_EXPIRED_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. The provider may already have accepted the message. The scheduler does not claim that row again, and the original sender cannot finalize the expired lease. Keep the row and its recorded outcome for review.

1. Check aggregate scheduler logs for reconciliation and dead-letter counts. `oldest_unknown_delivery_age_seconds` measures time since the oldest unresolved delivery was first queued. Use the affected organization's authorized operational access to confirm the state. Keep recipient addresses, report contents, and private identifiers out of shared tickets and logs.
2. Preserve the pending record and refer it to the named operator. Confirm provider acceptance using approved provider records, if available. If the evidence is inconclusive, maintain the unknown outcome. Provider acceptance alone does not establish recipient delivery.
3. Before any authorized replacement notification, recheck the current report version, recipient authority, and purpose. Operators record the outcome through the staff reconciliation endpoints: `GET /orgs/{org_uuid}/staff/report-deliveries/reconciliation` lists pending records, and `POST /orgs/{org_uuid}/staff/report-deliveries/{record_uuid}/reconciliation` applies a disposition of `accepted`, `known_unsent`, `suppressed` or `unknown` against an `expected_version`, with a required `Idempotency-Key` header and a bounded opaque `evidence_reference` (free text and URLs are rejected). Keep that path for the resolution; editing the database row directly or restarting the scheduler establishes neither provider acceptance nor recipient delivery and bypasses the retained decision. Follow the approved incident workflow when available.

The disposable PostgreSQL recovery test at `64cc4e5015dccde985ebe2a139efc944d9c2772d` passed all 13 concurrency tests. A fresh process handled an expired lease and the stale sender was refused. The lease deadline was simulated and no external provider was used. [Backup and restore evidence](MIGRATION-AND-ROLLBACK.md) covers the separate synthetic database rehearsal.

## Pilot cash reconciliation

The internal TASK-065 projection groups supported confirmed movements by organization and currency. Use the half-open period from the start timestamp up to, but excluding, the end timestamp. Collections and refunds use retained settlement dates. Chargebacks and reversals use their effective dates, including when they fall in a later period than the collection. Cash amounts are in minor units. Collections lacking manual settlement evidence are outside the projection, and tax and revenue figures are unavailable. An empty result means no supported confirmed movements in that scope.

The paid checkout regression on disposable PostgreSQL covers duplicate requests, a complete refund, a later chargeback reversal at the exact period boundary, and an empty ledger in a separate organization. The projection is read through the staff endpoints `GET /orgs/{org_uuid}/pilot/cash-report-offers` and `GET /orgs/{org_uuid}/pilot/offers/{offer_uuid}/versions/{offer_version_uuid}/confirmed-cash` (half-open `start`/`end`; responses are `private, no-store`), and the service layer requires the `FINANCIAL_RECONCILIATION` Studytomy capability in that organization. Finance authorization, approved reporting scope, and production acceptance are required before showing pilot figures.
